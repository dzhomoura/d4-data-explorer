# Chapter II — Stage 6A: Paladin glyph tooltip evidence

The 21 player-supplied Paladin glyph records match the pinned model at tooltip precision. All 11 Additional powers that call the threshold-bonus intrinsic match at a nominal value of `0.10`, and the other ten match their authored literal display parameters. All 21 Legendary readings match the rank-one curve with a level-51 floor. The existing Stage 4B and Stage 5B evaluators reproduce all 21 new Primary coefficients. These comparisons establish display agreement. Combat application, runtime binding, effective node contributions and stacking require evidence specific to those behaviors.

## Legendary previews and destinations

For the proposed tooltip model, use `effectiveLevel = Math.max(51, glyphLevel)` and `fraction = startingScalar + addedScalarPerLevel * (effectiveLevel - 1)`. Convert to percentage points with `100 * fraction` and round only for the one-decimal display. Below planner level 51 this evaluates a **locked preview**. The planner’s current level-derived Legendary rarity starts at 51. An applied planner modifier requires Legendary rarity and a purchased, configured socket. The authored rarity gate is independent of the Additional selector. The level-51 floor explains the low-level preview readings. It does not resolve the sequence of the level-50 material transaction, rarity transition and rank change in-game. ## Remaining runtime questions

- **Additional dispatch and composition.** The `[x]`, `[+]`, DR and event wording describes the effect presented to the player. The passive files carry no direct destination attributes that establish an engine bucket or operator. Challenger’s Additional and Legendary Elite bonuses are separate records. Their combined effect, along with intra-bucket and cross-bucket composition for the other powers, remains unmeasured.
- **Target effects and timed states.** Keep each trigger, target and beneficiary scope explicit. Canny and Chip use different trigger/beneficiary damage filters. Canny, Chip and Spirit show multiple tooltip increments while their extracted buff declares max stack 1. Actual persistence, stacking, target isolation, first-hit ordering, expiry and refresh behavior require controlled observation. Revenge’s authored stack reference still leaves refresh and application order open. Law needs Judging and repeat-application checks. Exploit needs per-enemy cooldown and trigger-order checks.
- **Predicates and special units.** Control’s alternatives are mutually exclusive in the tooltip, with simultaneous-condition precedence unmeasured. Arbiter’s elapsed-time counting and reset behavior, Fervor and Resolve cap interactions, DR composition, Undaunted’s Fortify-to-DR curve, Imbiber’s runtime potion-count inputs, Honed’s per-cast versus per-hit/per-target granularity, Resplendence’s cooldown event timing and Retribution’s size semantics remain open.
- **Legendary application and Primary precision.** Verify combat applicability for the authored Legendary destinations and the exact level-50 upgrade transaction. Keep the rank, displayed coefficient, native node, amplified node, socket, local requirement and global sheet readings distinct in future comparisons. Conditional Rare amplification remains unresolved under Stage 5B.
